## 2026-11-24: code audit of `bailingmoe3` in llama.cpp c661033 (read-only)

Predictions to confirm on the real files:
- Only `attn_k_b` (MLA, row width 128) is not a multiple of 256. 256-block types fall back for it: Q2_K/Q3_K → Q4_0, Q4_K → Q5_0, Q5_K → Q5_1, Q6_K → Q8_0, IQ1–IQ3 and IQ4_XS → IQ4_NL, even under `--tensor-type`.
- Never quantized: 1D tensors, norms, `ffn_gate_inp`, `ssm_conv1d*` (F32), `ssm_a`, `ssm_dt.bias`, `exp_probs_b`. Quantized although GLM5_NEXT protects the same kind: `ssm_f_a`, `ssm_g_a`, `ssm_beta`, `attn_kv_a_mqa`, `attn_k_b`, `attn_v_b`, `attn_gate`.
- Trap for "uniform" baselines: KDA q/k/v share the ATTENTION_Q/K/V category, so stock mixes move them (Q4_K_M bumps some KDA `attn_v` to Q6_K; IQ3_XXS drops KDA q/k to IQ2_S).
- `--tensor-type` uses unanchored `regex_search`, first match wins, and the pattern is lowercased (`\D \S \W` break). `attn_q` also hits `attn_q_a/_b`; anchor with `\.weight`.
- imatrix covers KDA projections and per-expert slices; unrouted experts get count 0 and quantize as uniform weights with only a "partial data" warning. `output.weight` needs `--process-output`; MTP needs `--nextn`, else IQ1–IQ3_XXS stop with "Missing importance matrix".
- KDA op `ggml_gated_delta_net` runs on Metal (S_v % 32 == 0), recurrent only, FP32 state; the token loop counter is a `short`, so keep `-ub` < 32768.
- 32K KLD: about 10 GB logits + 10 GB log-probs in RAM and a 5.15 GB base file per chunk (vocab 157184). One chunk only.
- No YaRN mscale in `kq_scale`; Air's optional 1M-token YaRN would be wrong. MTP exported by default; perplexity and KLD run the trunk only.

## 2026-11-24: Q0 reference parity — llama.cpp's `bailingmoe3` forward matches HF (numbers from `runs/parity/`)

- Reference: HF modeling code with `fla` Triton ops replaced by pure-PyTorch copies of fla-core 0.6.0's naive KDA / simple-GLA / short-conv / gated-norm (`scripts/fla_torch.py`, isolation tests in `scripts/test_fla_torch.py`). bf16 weights upcast per call, fp32 compute, 17.6 GB peak RSS. Sanity: wiki PPL 9.9, "capital of Italy is" → " Rome" (agent-reported).
- Logic test (measured; the synthetic-model builder was not kept): random 8-layer F32 copy of the architecture (6 KDA, 2 MLA, 16 experts), CPU, ubatch 16: mean KL 2e-10, top-1 100%, max |Δlogit| 5e-4 over 2000 tokens. KDA recurrence, conv/state carry across 125 ubatches, MLA, sigmoid+bias group routing and shared expert all match.
- Real BF16 GGUF vs HF (measured, `runs/parity/parity-*.txt`): short prompts on Metal mean KL 7e-7 to 2.5e-5, top-1 100%. Long 2000-token prompt (548 positions scored): Metal default ubatch mean KL 1.83e-3, p99 2.7e-2, top-1 98.9%; Metal ubatch 16 mean KL 9.4e-4, top-1 98.7%; CPU ubatch 16 KV f32 mean KL 3.1e-3, top-1 97.3%.
- Residual: per-layer error ~1e-6 through layer 2 (Metal ub16), ~2e-4 from the first MLA layer (f16 KV), then jumps at single positions where HF's router top-k margin is 1e-5..2e-6 (median 2e-3): an expert flips and the change propagates. Metal at ubatch ≥ 32 and CPU on BF16 (activations rounded to bf16) add ~1e-3 relative at layer 0.
- Consequence for KLD: llama.cpp BF16 has a floor of ~1e-3 KL vs fp32 HF on long text, and it depends on backend, `-ub` and KV type. Build reference and quants with the same backend, `-ub` and KV type; never mix. The stub fidelity was checked against fla-core 0.6.0 line by line: no math difference, precision details only.

## 2026-11-24: Q2 Q8_0 works; its KLD floor is 0.0075 and sits in the routed experts. Q4 imatrix covers every matrix but not every expert

- Q2 (measured): `llama-quantize Q8_0` 8.8 s, 1.8 GB RSS → 8,408,187,872 B, 8.52 BPW (311 Q8_0, 215 F32). Greedy "The capital of France is" → " Paris." (BF16 identical). KLD vs BF16, 16 × 512 wikitext-2 test, Metal, default `-ub`: mean 0.00749 ± 0.00037, p99 0.084, max 0.91, top-1 96.25%; PPL 15.98 vs BF16 15.95. Pipeline check: BF16 vs its own base mean KLD 0.000000, max 5e-5, top-1 100%.
- The floor is not in embeddings/output, KDA, MLA, shared experts or the dense layer: keeping each at BF16 on top of Q8_0 gives 0.0072 / 0.0069 / 0.0075 / 0.0078 / 0.0075 (all within ~1.5 SE of 0.0075, `results/ablations.tsv`). By elimination it is the routed experts (96% of weights), amplified by group-limited sigmoid routing near-ties (Q0). Consequence: at 16 × 512 the SE is ~0.0004, so single-group effects much smaller than ~0.001 are not resolvable without paired per-token stats or more chunks.
- Q4 (measured, `results/q4-imatrix.md`): `llama-imatrix` on BF16, 100 × 512 tokens of wikitext-2 train, 47 s, 16.4 GB RSS (~1,090 tok/s). 309 of 311 quantizable matrices get data; `token_embd` is a lookup and `output` needs `--process-output`. ## 2026-11-25: addendum, same-suffix drift test (for the Air looping reports)

- Why: Air users report looping at 5–15K tokens in llama.cpp, once at BF16. If llama.cpp's KDA state or MLA cache drifted from the reference with context, that would explain it and would invalidate every Air KLD against a llama.cpp reference.
- Design (`scripts/make_drift_prompts.py`, `hf_ref.py` with `ids`/`save_last`): 3 windows of wikitext-2 test, each scored on the same final 512 tokens after 1,024, 4,096 and 8,192 tokens of history. HF reference fp32 compute (pure-torch fla stubs, eager attention; 18.2 GB peak, ~87 s per 8K prompt); llama.cpp BF16 GGUF on CPU (f32 KV, ubatch 16) and on Metal (defaults). Safetensors re-downloaded at `9a98e35` for this.
- Result (measured, mean KL(HF ‖ GGUF) over the 3 windows): CPU 1K 3.97e-3, 4K 1.97e-3, 8K 2.82e-3; Metal 4.23e-3, 1.98e-3, 2.10e-3. Top-1 96–99% everywhere. No growth with history; the 1K histories score highest (shorter context, flatter predictions; inferred). Raw tables in `runs/drift/parity-*.txt`.
- Conclusion: on tiny, up to 8K, llama.cpp's KDA recurrence and MLA cache track the reference. Not covered: > 8K (eager HF attention at 16K would exceed the 30 GB rule), Air's SwiGLU-clamp layers (tiny has none), MTP, and free decoding (teacher-forced KL cannot see looping).

## 2026-11-25: gate T registered (before any refined build exists)

Question: does the full-Gram code refiner from `inkling-2-mini-quant` (`scripts/refine-codes.py`, Addendum H amendments 9, 9b, 9c) give a real KL gain on `bailingmoe3` routed experts, before anyone ports it to Ling-3.2-air (512 experts, 20,480 expert matrices at input dim 2560)?
- Builds (equal bytes by construction, only codes change): U3 = base Q8_0 with routed experts gate/up/down at IQ3_XXS (imatrix from the Gram capture), R3 = U3 with refined expert codes; U2/R2 the same at IQ2_XXS.
